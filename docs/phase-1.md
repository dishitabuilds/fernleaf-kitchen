## Implemented rules and choices

- **Catalogue:** dishes have SKU/name/description/image URL/temperature/cost/station/minimum quantity/activity and allergen/dietary joins. Options have independent cost/activity/guidance and are reused in ordered required/optional groups. Active flags retire records; historical IDs are retained. Portion-size references exist, while portion selections/surcharge matrices remain Phase 6 Should work.
- **Pricing:** USD cents fit PostgreSQL Int. Select `company.priceTierId ?? settings.defaultPriceTierId`; an explicit price wins in that tier. Otherwise manual means missing, cost means exact cost times ratio, and reference means the referenced resolved price times ratio. Derived `N/D` cents rounds as `5 * ceil(N/(5*D))`; 211 cents rounds to 215, 210 remains 210. A 211-cent cost at `115/100` resolves to 245 cents. Explicit 211/zero stay exact. Reference cycles are rejected even when an override could hide them. Clearing an override restores derivation/missing state.
- **Menu:** active category/item/dish, company hiding and resolved price must pass. Inactive/unpriced options are excluded. Every required group needs a valid priced option. Secret categories are absent from normal preview; direct links do not bypass company restrictions. Allergen intersections are warnings, and dietary tags/preferences are guidance; neither automatically filters food. Orders will reuse these backend rules in Phase 2.
- **Company identity:** domains normalize lowercase/IDNA and reject schemes, paths, email addresses, malformed labels and entries in the editable active public-provider denylist. At least one domain is required, and global uniqueness is enforced by PostgreSQL. This is not proof of domain ownership. No additional email/domain verification flow was added.
- **Ownership/defaults:** company creation includes owner employee and initial active address in one transaction. Owners/default addresses must belong to the same company and be active when selected. Drivers must be active Driver staff. Packaging must be an active packaging reference; company tier must be active. Retiring the current owner/default address requires replacement first.
- **Employees:** each has one required company, normalized email unique within that company, phone/activity, address/time/packaging flags and allergy/preference joins. A transfer of an owner needs another active source-company employee as replacement. Source owner/version and employee membership update atomically; a duplicate target email rolls back both. Flags are persisted now; crafted-order enforcement and immutable captured billing companies follow in Phase 2.
- **Calendars/settings:** day numbers are `0=Sunday` through `6=Saturday`; unique working days include at least one day, holiday dates must exist. Only company calendar determines allowed delivery dates; only kitchen calendar determines cutoff counting. Starting before delivery, count kitchen working days skipping holidays, then apply the kitchen time in Asia/Kolkata. Zero days uses delivery day. Cutoff count accepts 0–30, risk minutes 0–1440. Settings fixes USD/Asia/Kolkata and one active default tier.
- **Example cutoff:** delivery Wednesday 7 October 2026 with two days and 16:00 gives Monday 5 October at 16:00 IST. Marking Monday as a kitchen holiday with weekends closed gives Friday 2 October 16:00 IST. A company holiday on Wednesday makes delivery ineligible without shifting that kitchen cutoff. Preview calculates only; automatic/manual processing and order locks are Phase 2.
- **Concurrency:** company/settings versions reject stale saves. Mutations use serializable transactions with at most three attempts for serialization conflicts and database uniqueness/FKs. Active packaging/station usage blocks retirement; retiring guidance references preserves existing joins. Issued snapshots/invoices and readiness aggregates are future acceptance checks, not covered by configuration race success.
- **Seeds:** configuration installs atomically once when singleton settings is absent, then is skipped on later runs. This preserves edits and intentionally deleted overrides/group membership/domains. Staff seeding inserts missing demo accounts without overwriting accounts. The seed is not a database repair/reset tool. Initial synthetic data contains a seven-day company, two employees, address, three tiers, three dishes, one option/required group, normal/secret menus and all reference kinds. Operational rolling fixtures remain Phase 4/5.
